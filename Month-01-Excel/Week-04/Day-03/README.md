## 🛠️ Practical Work

Created summary tables and visualized sales data
to understand city-wise performance, monthly trends,
sales, orders and profit.

## 📊 Visualizations Created

- Sales by City
- Monthly Sales Trend
- City-wise Sales & Profit
- Sales Contribution by City
- KPI Summary
- Dashboard

## 📈 Summary Analysis

PivotTables were used to create aggregated
city-wise and month-wise summaries before
creating visualizations.

SUMIF/SUMIFS can also be used to create
such summary tables.

## 💡 Key Learning

The appropriate chart should be selected according
to the business question.

Comparison → Column / Bar
Trend → Line
Part of Whole → Pie

## 🔄 Analyst Workflow

Raw Data
→ Summary
→ Visualization
→ Business Insight

## 🎯 Main Learning

Data visualization is not just about making charts.
The goal is to present relevant information clearly
and make patterns, comparisons and trends easier
to understand.
