# Week 03 - Day 04: Business Analysis with PivotTables

## 📌 Topics Covered
- Business-oriented PivotTable analysis
- Sales performance analysis
- City-wise analysis
- Category-wise analysis
- Salesperson performance
- Product performance
- Multi-dimensional PivotTables
- Writing business observations and insights

## 🛠️ Practical Work

Used PivotTables to answer 10 business questions related to:
- City sales performance
- Category sales performance
- Salesperson performance
- Quantity analysis
- Category × City analysis
- Salesperson × Category analysis
- Product performance
- Order count
- Salesperson × City performance

## 💡 Key Learning

Learned to move beyond simply calculating numbers.

The analysis process followed:

Business Question → PivotTable → Result → Observation → Business Insight

A good analyst should explain what the numbers mean instead of only reporting them.

## 📊 Business Insight Practice

After creating PivotTables, I wrote observations explaining:
- What happened in the data
- Which category/city/person performed differently
- What the result could mean from a business perspective
